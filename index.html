How to brew it

  1. 1
    Heat the waterBoil the kettle, then leave it for about a minute. Green tea tastes best around 80°C, not at a full boil.
  2. 2
    Add one bag to a mugPour in about 250 mL. Use one bag per mug.
  3. 3
    Steep for 3 minutesLeave it long enough to draw out the flavour, and short enough to keep it from turning bitter.
  4. 4
    Remove the bag and start your sessionDrink it warm before you sit down to study or work.
